● climate-data-download

Download climate data

Route any climate variable to the right source — CHIRPS, CHIRTS-ERA5, NASA POWER, or AgERA5 — and fetch it automatically.

What it does
  • Precipitation from CHIRPS (daily, 0.05°)
  • Tmax / Tmin from CHIRTS-ERA5
  • Solar radiation and humidity from NASA POWER
  • Wind speed, VPD, Ref. ET from AgERA5
CHIRPS CHIRTS-ERA5 NASA POWER AgERA5 xarray

● geospatial-cube-processor

Process spatial cubes

Clip rasters to admin boundaries, stack multi-source datasets to a common grid, and compute zonal statistics per admin unit.

What it does
  • Mask to country / province / district (GADM v4.1)
  • Stack CHIRPS + CHIRTS + NASA POWER into one cube
  • Compute mean / sum / max per admin unit
  • Export monthly / seasonal / annual CSVs
rioxarray geopandas GADM zonal stats xarray
cube = stack_datasets([chirps, chirts, power], target_resolution="coarsest") eth = mask_to_admin(cube, "ETH", admin_level=1) df = summarize_by_admin(eth, boundaries, agg_method="mean", temporal_freq="ME")
